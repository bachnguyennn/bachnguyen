---
title: "When Simple Models Win"
description: "Why baseline-first modeling often beats jumping straight to complex architectures."
pubDate: 2026-02-14
tags: ["Modeling", "Baselines", "Practical ML"]
---

Complex models are valuable, but they are not automatically better.

Strong baseline models often reveal:

- whether the signal is actually present
- which features carry most of the value
- where complexity creates maintenance cost without clear gains
