## Problem
Manual analysis across option expirations made relative mispricing opportunities hard to detect quickly.

## Approach
Built a pipeline to normalize chain data and highlight cross-expiration spreads with live risk signals.

## Result
Reduced screening time and improved consistency in evaluating opportunity quality.
